# Swiftlane

> Swiftlane is a cloud-based access control and video intercom platform designed for modern multifamily residential and commercial buildings. The company's mission is "Creating Safer Communities" by combining high security with everyday convenience through touchless, mobile-first technology.

## Company

- **Website**: https://swiftlane.com
- **Twitter / X**: @swiftlaneaccess
- **Location**: San Francisco, CA
- **Market**: Physical security — access control and video intercom for multifamily residential and commercial properties

## Products

- **Video Intercom**: Cloud-connected intercom for building entry; supports two-way video, remote unlock, and visitor management
- **Face Recognition Access**: Touchless entry using 3D depth sensing with anti-spoofing; no card or phone required
- **Mobile Access**: Smartphone-based credentials replacing keycards and fobs; Bluetooth and remote unlock
- **Key Card / Fob Access**: Traditional RFID credentials managed from the cloud dashboard
- **Visitor PIN Access**: Temporary one-time or recurring PINs for guests, couriers, and service providers
- **Vehicle Reader**: License-plate or long-range reader for parking and gate access
- **Cloud Management Dashboard**: Centralized access log, user management, permissions, and remote control for all access points
- **Health Check**: Proactive system monitoring and diagnostics tool for installed hardware

## Target Customers

- Multifamily property managers and owners (apartments, condos, HOAs)
- Commercial building owners and office managers
- Security installers and integrators
- Enterprise organizations with multi-site access needs

## Key Differentiators

- End-to-end system: one platform covers every access point in a building
- Touchless / contactless entry (face recognition, mobile)
- Cloud-first architecture — no on-premise server required
- Multi-credential support: face, mobile, card, PIN, and vehicle in one system
- Video intercom with remote access management
